# Chupetón

**A modpack made for playing with friends.** Tech, exploration, combat and chaos in one world, built together (or blown up together).

## ⚙️ Tech with Create

Create plus a pile of addons: machines, trains, jetpacks, mechanical weapons, automated enchanting, industrial decor and even wine making.

## 🗺️ Exploration

Overhauled terrain with **Terralith** and **Tectonic**, new biomes from **Biomes O' Plenty** and **Regions Unexplored**, and all of **YUNG's** improved structures: dungeons, temples, mineshafts and fortresses. To get around you have **Waystones**, **Xaero's** maps, compasses that find biomes and structures, and wings to fly.

## ⚔️ Combat

**Better Combat** with animations and combos, new weapons, and per-player loot with **Lootr**, so everyone gets their own chest.

## 🍖 Life in the world

**Farmer's Delight** for cooking, new animals, easier villager management, trees that fall in one chop, gravestones so you keep your stuff when you die, and a **casino** to lose it all anyway.

## 🚀 Performance

**Sodium**, **Iris** (shaders), **C2ME**, **ModernFix**, **FerriteCore** and more, so it runs smoothly even with 100+ mods.

***

**Version:** Minecraft 1.21.1  
**Mods:** 118  
**Best for:** a server with friends 🍼
